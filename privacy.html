Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Cyceme ("we", "our") provides a local pet growth journal on your device. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Data we store on your device

Pet profiles, timeline photos and clips, care records, generated growth pages, and Page Credit balances are stored locally in the app container. We do not require an account.

Permissions

  • Camera and Photo Library: used when you capture or choose media, or save generated pages.
  • Microphone and Speech Recognition: used only when you tap Speak Message.
  • App Tracking Transparency: requested on first Home visit to measure advertising performance. Core features work if you decline.
  • Notifications: requested after ATT on first Home visit. This build does not schedule or send notifications after the prompt.

Purchases

Page Credit packs are consumable In-App Purchases processed by Apple. Purchase receipts are handled by StoreKit on device.
