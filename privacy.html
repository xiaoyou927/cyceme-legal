Privacy Policy

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the Cyceme iPhone app (“Cyceme”, “the app”) handles information when you use the pet growth journal features on your device. Cyceme is built for local journaling: pet profiles, timeline media, care notes, and growth page images.

2. No account requirement

Cyceme does not require you to register an account, provide an email address, or sign in with a social network to use core features. The journal content you create is stored in the app container on your device.

3. Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use the app, Cyceme may store:

  • Pet profile details such as name, breed or notes, birthday or days-together values, and avatar images
  • Timeline posts including photos, short video clips, poster frames, captions, and timestamps
  • Care records such as weight, water, food amounts, vaccine or deworm notes, and related history
  • Generated growth page image files created after you confirm generation
  • Page Credit balance and a local purchase or consumption ledger for credits on this device
  • Simple preference flags such as whether onboarding is complete

This information remains on your device unless you export or share it yourself through the system share sheet or similar tools you choose to use.

4. Permissions you control

Cyceme requests permissions only when needed for a feature you start:

  • Camera — when you take a photo for a pet avatar or timeline post
  • Photo Library — when you choose an existing photo or video, or save media where the system requires access
  • Microphone — only when you tap Speak Message to dictate a caption
  • Speech Recognition — only with Speak Message, so spoken words can be turned into text

If you deny a permission, Cyceme continues to work for other features that do not need it. You can change permissions later in iOS Settings.

5. Speech recognition

Speak Message is optional. When you use it, audio may be processed by Apple’s speech recognition services so the app can fill the message field with text. Apple acts as the processor for that recognition request. You can refuse the permission and type captions instead. Cyceme does not operate a separate voice-upload server of its own for this feature.

6. System prompts on first Home visit

On the first visit to Home after getting started, iOS may show system prompts for App Tracking Transparency and notifications. These prompts are presented by the system. You may allow or deny them. Core journaling features (pets, timeline, care logs, and page generation with available credits) remain available if you decline.

7. Purchases

Page Credit packs are consumable In-App Purchases handled by Apple StoreKit. Apple processes payment and related receipt information under Apple’s privacy and media services terms. Cyceme records the local credit grant on your device after a verified successful purchase so you can spend credits to generate growth pages.

8. Data we do not sell

Cyceme does not sell your personal information. Pet photos, care notes, and journal text are not offered for sale to data brokers.

9. Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete individual posts, clear all data in Settings, or remove the app from your device. Clearing all data permanently deletes pets, timeline media, care records, generated pages, and remaining Page Credits stored in the app on that device.

10. Children

Cyceme is intended for general audiences who manage a personal pet journal. It is not directed at children under 13 as a primary audience. Do not submit information about a child if you are not authorized to do so.

11. International use

Because journal content is stored on your device, processing location follows where you use the device. Optional speech recognition, when enabled, is handled under Apple’s service regions and policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when features or legal requirements change. The current version is published at this URL and is opened inside Cyceme with an in-app browser view, the same way Settings and the welcome screen open it.
