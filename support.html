Common tips

  • Permissions are requested only when you use Camera, Photos, Microphone, or Speech actions.
  • ATT and Notifications prompts appear once on first Home entry after Get Started.
  • Preview never spends credits; Confirm Generate spends 1 Page Credit.
  • Clear All Data in Settings permanently deletes local content and remaining credits.
